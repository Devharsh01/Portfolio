> ## Short Explanation

Propellent is a comprehensive business website developed for a software startup, designed to establish their professional online presence and effectively showcase their services to potential clients. The platform serves as a digital storefront that communicates the company's expertise and value proposition.

> ## Project Goals

The Propellent website was designed with specific business objectives:

- Establish a professional online presence for the software startup
- Clearly communicate services and capabilities to potential clients
- Generate leads and inquiries from interested prospects
- Build credibility and trust in the competitive software market
- Create a scalable platform that can grow with the business
- Differentiate the startup from competitors through superior web presence

- **Tailwind CSS**: Utility-first CSS framework for rapid, consistent styling
- **Framer Motion**: Advanced animation library for engaging user interactions
